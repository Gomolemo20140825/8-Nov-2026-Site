What I can help with

Six ways to move a decision forward.

Engagements are shaped around the question, the available evidence and the people who need to make the call.

01 · Assess

Technical due diligence

Assess process, materials and technology assumptions that matter to an investment or asset decision.

Who it’s for · How I help
Who
Investors, operators, leadership teams
How
Review evidence, surface risks and clarify decision-critical unknowns
Why
Make technical risks and opportunities visible before committing capital
02 · Commercialise

R&D commercialisation

Connect an innovation opportunity to a product, customer and business case that can move toward implementation.

Who it’s for · How I help
Who
R&D teams, product owners, commercial leaders
How
Link technical requirements, customer needs, economics and stage-gate decisions
Why
Bridge the gap between a promising result and a fundable launch
03 · Improve

Process, energy and cost optimisation

Identify ways to improve operations while keeping quality, process constraints and financial impact in view.

Who it’s for · How I help
Who
Manufacturing and operations teams
How
Understand process performance, quantify options and prioritise practical changes
Why
Translate operational improvement into measurable business value
04 · Align

Sustainability strategy and ESG

Bring sustainability criteria into portfolio and business decisions with an eye to standards and market opportunity.

Who it’s for · How I help
Who
Strategy, sustainability and product teams
How
Connect relevant criteria, portfolio context and commercial implications
Why
Make sustainability actionable for both compliance and growth
05 · Evaluate

Equity and market analysis

Apply finance and industry understanding to market questions and investment perspectives.

Who it’s for · How I help
Who
Investors and business decision-makers
How
Frame the question, examine available market information and test assumptions
Why
Put industrial market context into a structured decision
06 · Share

Board and speaking engagements

Bring an engineering-to-finance perspective to leadership conversations, events and audiences.

Who it’s for · How I help
Who
Boards, leadership groups and event organisers
How
Shape a discussion around the audience and the decision or idea at hand
Why
Help connect technical change to strategic and financial outcomes
