Who I am

A technical leader with a financial lens.

I’m Itumeleng D. Kgongwane, a chemical engineer from Wits, a finance graduate of Fordham Gabelli, and an MBA graduate of the Gordon Institute of Business Science (GIBS). I’m based in Pretoria, South Africa.

My career has taken me from process engineering at BASF and Sappi to leading research and product innovation. I currently manage a team of scientists across manufacturing, process and product innovation at Sappi R&D.

I use engineering, finance, and AI and machine learning to help lift efficiency, cut quality defects and reduce fixed costs. The work I care about most is making the handover from lab to mill floor—and from technical result to fundable business case—clear and practical.

My experience spans chemical engineering, R&D, corporate finance, sustainability and business development. I’m a member of the Institute of Directors South Africa and contribute to Sappi’s Global Sustainability Task Force.

How I think
“The best technical solution is one people can understand, execute and invest in.”

Connect the science. Make the economics visible. Help teams move forward.
