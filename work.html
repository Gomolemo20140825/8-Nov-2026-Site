CS/02 · 2023–present · Advanced materialsR203M

MFC scale-up from bagasse pulp

Who Sappi R&D, Stanger Mill and Sappi Europe R&DWhere Stanger Mill · South Africa
Challenge, approach and result

The challenge

Turning South African pulps into microfibrillated cellulose (MFC) required identifying the right energy input and scaling the process at mill level.

What I did

Worked with Sappi Europe R&D to establish the process conditions and scale MFC using bagasse pulp at Stanger Mill.

Why it mattered

Bagasse MFC offsets raw-material input at approximately R1,000/t without compromising quality, adding R24M EBITDA a year.

R203M · 15-year NPVR24M EBITDA · per year
CS/03 · 2024–2025 · Business development+141%

MFC in corrugator starch adhesive

Who Sappi R&D and corrugator customersWhen Opportunity development and customer trials · 2024–2025
Challenge, approach and result

The challenge

Corrugating adhesive variability, bond strength and speed needed a validated recipe and a business case strong enough to justify customer trials.

What I did

Identified the opportunity, sized TAM/SAM/SOM, built a pricing model and won executive buy-in. Customer trials tested the economics on heavier boards.

Why it mattered

Trials showed a 141% increase in bond strength and 60% lower process variability.

CS/04 · 2013–2017 · Mill operationsR20M

Filler chemistry and water efficiency

Who Sappi Enstra mill operationsWhere Springs, South Africa
Challenge, approach and result

The challenge

Imported pulp fibre was costly and exposed to exchange-rate risk, while daily water, steam and energy use lacked visibility.

What I did

Applied filler-increase chemistry to substitute costly fibre and built a quasi-dynamic dashboard tracking water, steam and energy daily.

Why it mattered

Cost savings reached R20M and water use decreased from 15 to 9 ML/day.

R20M cost savings15 → 9 ML/day water
